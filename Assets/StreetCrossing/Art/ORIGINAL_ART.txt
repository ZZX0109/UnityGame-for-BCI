Original Street Crossing modular art

The accompanying sedan, hatchback, van, wheel and residential module OBJ meshes,
prefabs and asphalt/paving/plaster albedo and normal textures were authored for
this project with the included deterministic build_original_art.py source.
They contain no downloaded vehicle/building models or stock image textures.
Mesh coordinates are metres; the three vehicle bodies measure 4.2, 3.7 and 4.8 m.
Standard shader material parameters are artistic prototype choices.

The original procedural sound generation is in StreetAudioController.cs.
The separate Street UI Sans font is derived from Noto Sans CJK under SIL OFL 1.1;
see Resources/StreetCrossing/Fonts/LICENSE.txt for that font's full attribution.

Visual refinement authored 2026-11-13:
StreetDetailMesh.cs generates the rounded signal/furniture silhouettes at runtime;
StreetVisualDetails.cs authors the recessed market displays, shelter timetable,
slatted bench, drain details and three district landmarks. StreetVisualProfile
assets select original colours and layouts. TrafficSignalView.cs authors the
standing/walking pedestrian icons and circular lenses. These original runtime
meshes are not outputs of build_original_art.py and require no downloaded assets.
The original art generator remains the source of existing OBJ/texture/prefab files.
All new street decoration has no physics/visibility colliders. Existing shop
window collider envelopes remain physical opaque boundaries despite recessed
window-display rendering; no see-through glass is introduced.
