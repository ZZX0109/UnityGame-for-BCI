## Unity mapping and mobile import

- `Asphalt.mat` uses the albedo JPG as sRGB, the OpenGL normal as a linear normal map, and the generated smoothness-alpha PNG as linear metallic/gloss input.
- The Standard shader expects smoothness in `_MetallicGlossMap.a`; the PNG is generated as `alpha = 255 - roughness`, with RGB set to zero. The source roughness JPEG remains alongside it unchanged. Generated PNG SHA-256: BA2069DA09AC8DFBD476C30679B5A0B5A0A4D55F64DB8C8779ED4C90AC72B750.
- Mipmaps are enabled; Android texture overrides use ASTC 6x6 at max 2048, anisotropic filtering 8, trilinear filtering. Normal and data maps are linear.
- Asphalt UVs in the main road, route aprons, and distant continuation use root-local metres divided by `StreetWorld.AsphaltTileSizeMetres` (3.0), with material texture scale 1.
