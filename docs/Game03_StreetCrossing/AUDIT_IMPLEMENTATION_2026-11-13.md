## Play and inspect

Open `Assets/StreetCrossing/Scenes/StreetCrossingScene.unity`. In the Editor run **Street Crossing → Prepare/Validate Delivery** (or `StreetCrossing.Editor.StreetSceneBuilder.PrepareForValidation`) first. The preparation step verifies shared TMP resources and resolves imported OBJ Mesh subasset references through Unity, preserving actual importer IDs. The single entry scene presents S01, S02 and S03 cards and rebuilds the selected scenario in place. The original PuzzleScene remains first and enabled in project Build Settings.

WASD continuously walks; mouse turns the actual camera; Shift enables bounded fast walk; E inspects a nearby visible sign; Esc pauses; R requests restart confirmation. At the tactile kerb, look toward the approaching lane on each side long enough to record visibility. A queued vehicle can itself be a valid observation target. A shelter/building between camera and target blocks it. Looking upward/backward, looking outside FOV or trying after entry cannot complete the requirement. In S03 move forward inside the safe waiting area to clear the left occlusion. Observe the world signals and actual cars, then use a Walk window to reach the opposite shop mat.

All scenarios use one six-phase signal authority. Amber commitments are latched; Walk waits for committed cars' complete rear clearance, including upstream turns. S03 separately reserves the side-road merge junction: turning traffic yields until straight traffic can safely stop outside it, conflicting turns wait, and merged cars continue to obey physical following gaps even with different route IDs. The protected crossing remains closed to new conflicting admissions during pedestrian priority. A player elsewhere on the roadway is evaluated as unsafe and sensed locally by the relevant vehicle, rather than globally controlling the entire street.

Stationary vehicle contact records a contact without instantly ending the attempt. A physical moving vehicle hit ends it with a quiet explanatory result; dangerous closing encounters are separate. Both observation requirements are enabled in the supplied assets but are configurable independently. Time is descriptive, never a speed reward. Observation is camera visibility, not eye gaze or a clinical metric.

## Assets and remaining limits

All model/texture/procedural audio source is original; see `Art/ORIGINAL_ART.txt` and `tools/build_original_art.py`. Street UI Sans is a renamed SC subset of Noto Sans CJK2.004 with full SIL OFL1.1 retained. TMP3.0.6 is pinned consistently with Unity2022.3 official package guidance. Shared TMP assets are identical across both game branches and introduce no game-specific default font.

This remains a bounded, semi-realistic desktop prototype: traffic phase timings are configurable game parameters, not a regional traffic-law standard. Turning routes use kinematic paths and conservative reservations rather than WheelCollider simulation. Assets are lightweight original low-poly modules and procedural material maps; their visual realism is not claimed to have been inspected in Unity here. NPC pedestrians, S04/S05 and real hardware/clinical validation are outside this uploaded audit's requested S01–S03 implementation.
